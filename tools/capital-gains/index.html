This is the most rule-sensitive tool here

Capital gains rules were substantially amended with effect from 2024-07-23. Holding periods, rates and the availability of indexation all changed, and transitional treatment applies to some assets acquired before that date.

This tool computes the gain and applies the rate configured for the asset type. It does not apply exemptions on reinvestment, set-off of losses, treatment of inherited or gifted assets, or the position of a non-resident. The indexation comparison is disabled because the cost inflation index has not been populated in content/tax-rates.json — the tool declines to guess.

Indicative only

These calculators produce indicative results based only on the figures you enter and the assumptions stated on each page. They are not professional advice and must not be relied on for filing, planning or any other purpose. Tax law changes frequently. Please speak to us about your specific circumstances.

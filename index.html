Who we are

A firm built around judgement

Sawant & Kadrekar LLP was established in 1986 by CA Rajendra Kadrekar and CA Bajarang Sawant. Four decades on, it is a multi-disciplinary practice of eight partners working from six offices across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Virar and Pune.

Compliance is the floor. What distinguishes an adviser is the quality of the judgement applied where the rules stop short - in a position that has to be defended, a control that has to be relied on, a number that has to hold up in diligence. We are structured so that judgement is not delegated.

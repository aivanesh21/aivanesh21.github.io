Industries

Sector context changes the answer

The same transaction can carry very different accounting, tax and regulatory consequences depending on the sector it sits in. These are the areas we are most often asked about.

Manufacturing

Inventory valuation, cost accounting, capital allowances and an input tax credit chain that runs across states.

Learn more

Technology & SaaS

Revenue recognition over time, export of services, ESOP accounting and cross-border withholding.

Learn more

Financial Services

Regulatory reporting, expected credit loss, capital adequacy and a control environment under close supervision.

Learn more

Real Estate & Construction

Percentage-of-completion judgements, project-wise accounting, RERA obligations and joint development structuring.

Learn more

Retail & Consumer

High transaction volumes, multi-state registrations, e-commerce operator obligations and inventory shrinkage controls.

Learn more

Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals

Exempt and taxable supply mix, research capitalisation, regulatory compliance and distributor arrangements.

Learn more

Professional & Business Services

Work-in-progress recognition, partner remuneration structures, cross-border service tax and engagement profitability.

Learn more

Not-for-Profit & Social Sector

Registration and exemption conditions, foreign contribution rules, grant utilisation reporting and donor assurance.

Learn more

NBFCs & Lending

Scale-based classification, IRAC norms, KYC obligations, loan file integrity and a supervisory regime that keeps tightening.

Learn more

Capital Markets & Asset Management

Listing obligations, continuous disclosure, intermediary compliance and fund-level reporting.

Learn more

Infrastructure & EPC

Long-cycle contract accounting, retention and claims, joint ventures, and cash that arrives long after the work.

Learn more

Logistics, Shipping & Ports

Place-of-supply complexity, cross-border documentation, freight and demurrage recognition, and asset-heavy balance sheets.

Learn more

Media & Entertainment

Content amortisation, royalty and residual accounting, withholding on cross-border rights, and revenue that arrives in tranches.

Learn more

Education & EdTech

Exemption conditions for institutions, deferred fee revenue, and the very different treatment of commercial edtech.

Learn more

Hospitality & Travel

Rate-linked GST, aggregator collections, loyalty liabilities and highly seasonal working capital.

Learn more

Chemicals & Specialty Materials

Process costing, by-product and yield accounting, environmental provisioning and an input credit chain across states.

Learn more

Co-operative & Housing Societies

Statutory audit under co-operative law, mutuality, redevelopment transactions and member-facing governance.

Learn more

Gems & Jewellery

Inventory valuation and physical control, hallmarking and PMLA obligations, gold loan and metal exchange arrangements.

Learn more

Energy & Renewables

Long-term power purchase agreements, project finance covenants, accelerated allowances and open access compliance.

Learn more

E-commerce & D2C

Marketplace collections, multi-state registrations, returns and cancellation accounting, and contribution economics that move weekly.

Learn more
